Reading:

* The deadline holds: the slowest call ended at 85.04 s, inside the 85 s
  deadline plus scheduling. No call ran past it.
* At 40 s, 2.5 % of attempts timed out, against 15–33 % at 20 s in lead smoke
  L3 (`lead_smoke_time_20261005.md`): most of the provider's slow tail finishes
  between 20 and 40 s.
* 2 of 55 calls failed after two consecutive slow attempts. In the service
  this is answered 503 + Retry-After before anything is persisted (the
  exception is the provider's timeout, which `encode_or_unavailable` maps to
  503; a deadline that leaves no time for a retry raises `DeadlineExceeded`,
  also 503). The pre-registration listed "outcome other than ok/deadline" as a
  defect to report: these two are the provider-timeout variant of the same 503,
  not a different behaviour.
* 0.45 Adds/s is the embedding-bound rate for BEAM-sized Adds (≈ 20k
  characters each), excluding extraction; L3 measured 1.15–1.61 requests/s of
  10 texts at c16, i.e. ≈ 0.25–0.35 Adds/s for 44 texts, with sequential
  batches.
* Shared provider, one run, time of day not controlled.
